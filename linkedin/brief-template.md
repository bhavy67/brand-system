# Post brief

Copy this into a new file in `posts/` as `<yyyy-mm-dd>-<slug>.md` and fill it in before drafting. If "Source" is empty, there's no post yet.

```markdown
---
date: yyyy-mm-dd
status: idea | draft | ready | published
type: concept | debug-story | decision | build-log | correction | observation | career | opinion | human | mixed
hook_shape: moment | plain-claim | wrong-belief | detail | constraint | result | code | admission | question-asked
ending: takeaway | position | question | quiet-stop
image_mode: A-brand | B-diagram | C-carousel | D-photo | E-screenshot | F-generated-real | G-generated-creative
words: 0
---

## Source
What actually happened, or what I actually think. Rough notes, in my words.

## The one idea
One sentence. If I can't write it, the post isn't ready.

## The only-me detail
The specific detail nobody else could write.

## Who it's for
The reader who should stop scrolling for this.

## Confidential check
Anything about clients, employers, code or data I must leave out or change?

## Draft
Hook

Context

End line

## Image
Mode, and what it shows. For generated images, the prompt.

## Checklist
- [ ] Real and defensible
- [ ] Only-me detail present
- [ ] 100 to 150 words
- [ ] Hook shape differs from last post
- [ ] Slop scan clean
- [ ] Image check passed (labelled if realistic generated)
```
