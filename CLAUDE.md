# Brand system: instructions for Claude

This repo defines Bhavy Ladani's visual and written identity across everything he publishes: profile photo, logo, covers, blog images, diagrams, post graphics, carousels, LinkedIn post ideas. Monochrome, light, minimal, polished, neutral and natural.

- Visuals of any kind: use the `brand-visuals` skill (`skills/brand-visuals/SKILL.md`), `DESIGN_RULES.md` and `tokens/tokens.css`. Start from `templates/`.
- Writing, captions and post ideas: use the `brand-content` skill (`skills/brand-content/SKILL.md`) and `CONTENT_RULES.md`.
- LinkedIn posts (writing plus image): use the `linkedin-post` skill (`skills/linkedin-post/SKILL.md`) and `linkedin/`. Posts are 100 to 150 words: hook, context, end line, image. Generated images are allowed for LinkedIn posts only, under the rules in `linkedin/images.md`.
- Never produce AI-slop: no gradients, glows, stock-AI imagery, buzzwords, emoji, machine-sounding phrasing or invented metrics.
- Do not add Claude or AI attribution (Co-Authored-By trailers, "Generated with" lines) to commits, PRs or files in this repo.
